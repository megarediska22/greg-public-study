# Fast Adaptive Placement · Method and implementation contract

**Method `fast-placement-v1`.** The existing print and text criteria remain the course's vocabulary for deciding what to practice. Use a separate set of placement signals for the fast session. The earlier R/P diagnostic rules and existing practice, checkpoint and skip decisions retain their meaning. Independent writing is verified later in normal learning. This specification governs the fast local pilot. [Prior evidence rules](../../research/curriculum-assessment/assessment/EVIDENCE_RULES.md) · [Prior placement specification](../../research/curriculum-assessment/assessment/PLACEMENT_SPEC.md)

## Signals and learning follow-up

| Signal | Meaning | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R** | Choose an answer, sentence or missing word. | Recognition or discrimination within the presented options. |
| **S** | Construct a bounded answer using supplied word cards. | Supported construction, such as choosing and ordering the words of a question. |
| **P** | Independently produce an answer without supplied options or words. | Available in ordinary learning under existing evidence rules. Fast placement never produces P. |

Accept a sign from the first unaided submitted response to an eligible item and count one sign per independent family and criterion. A skip, “Не знаю”, unfinished selection, missing/withdrawn key or ambiguous attribution has no sign. Negative signs require a clear error on the target. For example, card order errors in a multi-clause response may be ambiguous. An incorrect selection caused by that ambiguity stays neutral. R and S must remain distinct even where either can support a route decision. Never transform S or two R signs into P or write fast signals into the fold that controls independent course knowledge, lesson skip or checkpoint readiness.

Keep a fresh summary per criterion. With zero signs show insufficient evidence. One sign is limited evidence. Two independent agreements strengthen preliminary positive or negative evidence. Opposed signs are disputed. In a three-sign group, a final pair of same-sign independent responses after the opposed sign can restore a preliminary direction only if the last item is a deliberately selected clarifier. Otherwise retain disputed. Denote effective positive, negative and disputed group states below as +, − and ±. A single positive can support an anchor when required alongside independent observations of other criteria. A single negative never triggers a gap recommendation. Older independent knowledge, previous starts and access remain visible as history. Fresh fast signals determine a new fast recommendation. Never count an old answer or a completed lesson as a fresh sign.

During ordinary learning, continue existing practice observations. Prioritize a narrow ordinary task on an uncertain prerequisite when available and unobtrusive. Two qualifying signs including genuine P can confirm that skill under the existing rules. If the course supplies no suitable P, leave it preliminary. Revision after a later observed difficulty yields optional practice advice and preserves issued grants and completed lessons.

## Route and stopping rules

Count presented items once. The user can pause or finish at any point. Automatic shortest route is 2 items for clear print difficulty. A supported early A1 route can finish in 7. Expected useful horizontal routes take 10–12, with a hard cap of 15. Exhausted eligible material can end a route sooner with explicit uncertainty. The clock is observational and never affects recommendations.

1. **Entry.** Present one LAT item. If it yields an attributable negative, present the independent LAT follow-up immediately. Two clear negatives give elective Pre-A1 and finish. Otherwise proceed. The first five core slots in fixed order are LAT, BE, ASK, ORIENT and NOTE. Use ASK as S and the other core items as R. The ASK foundation follow-up also uses S in a different context. Use varied everyday contexts. The LAT follow-up, when taken, is in addition to the core five.
2. **Change difficulty.** After core, serve the upper block if LAT, BE and ASK are all + and at least one of ORIENT/NOTE is +. Serve **NUM, CONSTRAINT, WRITE (S), a second CONSTRAINT in an independent context, and TRANSFER** in that order. TRANSFER is one alternate A2 reading probe (GIST or MEDIATE), contributes only to the explanation, and does not grant the final A2 checkpoint. If not eligible for upper, serve three contrasting short contexts in order **NUM, BE follow-up, ASK follow-up**.
3. **Clarify or finish.** On the foundation branch, check the targeted early A1 profile after each BE or ASK follow-up. Finish when that profile is satisfied. The earliest such stop is item 7 for two BE negatives, or item 8 for two ASK negatives with the BE prerequisite. Otherwise use at most two further foundation items to reach ten when eligible material can improve the decision. On the upper branch finish after its five probes if the criteria support the A2 route or no additional eligible probe can change the recommendation. Otherwise serve at most two targeted clarifications. On either branch clarify in order LAT, BE, ASK, ORIENT, NOTE, NUM, CONSTRAINT, WRITE. Pick the first missing, disputed or undersampled prerequisite that could change a reachable route given the remaining slots. A single positive in CONSTRAINT is undersampled for the later A2 route. An ASK or WRITE clarifier that supplies a route prerequisite uses S. Use a different operation or stimulus family. At most one optional clarifier per criterion, and never use a clarifier to chase two already clear negatives. If no informative eligible slot exists, finish with the evidence present. Freeze the presented count at 15 regardless of path.

A missing criterion can be improved by one positive. If it is disputed, a single clarifier can restore a direction only under the rule above. A branch failure, invalid item or unavailable fresh family cannot fabricate evidence. The number of optional items in each route is deliberately bounded so a fatigue run cannot become a longer exam. For the technical form contract and acceptance matrix see [HANDOFF_05.md](handoffs/HANDOFF_05.md).

## Result, from strongest supported option downward

| Result | Fresh support | Primary or optional action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elective Pre-A1 | Two direct LAT negatives, distinct families | Recommend `bp-pre-01` and offer A1 or prior resume. |
| Targeted early A1 | Two direct BE negatives with LAT +, or two direct ASK negatives with LAT and BE + | Offer `to-be` or `do-does` respectively. Show supported later skills separately. Returning learners retain resume as the primary action when practice would move them earlier. |
| A2 · later entry | A2 entry profile plus two independent CONSTRAINT positives and WRITE S positive | Offer `unit-062` as a provisional text-based start. |
| A2 · entry | A2 entry profile and no sufficient later A2 evidence | Offer `unit-042` as provisional entry to A2. Explain which A2 criteria remain unclear. |
| Late A1 | LAT, BE and ASK all + | Offer `hours-and-signs`. State if ORIENT/NOTE or NUM lack sufficient evidence for A2 entry. |
| Insufficient | Missing or disputed anchor evidence without a supported result | Show what is known, then offer resume where available, A1 as a voluntary choice, elective Pre-A1 where relevant, or another fresh form if available. |

The A2 entry profile for `unit-042` requires LAT, BE, ASK, NUM, ORIENT and NOTE all +. The “A2 · entry” row uses that profile. A prepared learner leaving early after a supported profile may accept that preliminary recommendation. A learner leaving after many skips retains their usable fresh evidence and any valid resume action. Never assign the first lesson as a new diagnostic recommendation simply because the data are sparse. Do not offer an unavailable catalog ID or an unsupported higher anchor. If the best-supported target is unavailable, use the next supported ready target or the explicit elective choices. A placement choice grants only its selected lesson. Ordinary course progression after that lesson uses existing rules.
